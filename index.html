FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Lift'd free?

Yes, completely. Create an account, join your gym, and start connecting in under a minute.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Lift'd runs in your browser on any phone or computer. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon. Native iPhone and Android apps are on the way.

My gym isn't on Lift'd yet. Now what?

Start it! Add your gym in a few seconds and you'll get an invite link to share wherever your gym's people already are: group chats, Instagram, or the folks you see every day. Every gym's community starts with one founding member.

I'm new to lifting. Is Lift'd for me?

Definitely. Short clips show you how to do each lift, you can filter them to beginner level, and your gym's community is a place to ask questions and find someone to train with.

Is my information private and safe?

Your workouts and food log are private to you. Your profile, posts, and gym chat are visible to signed-in members, and you can report or block anyone in a tap.
