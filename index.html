FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Lift'd free?

Yes, completely. Create an account, join your gym, and start connecting in under a minute.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Lift'd runs in your browser on any phone or computer. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon. Native iPhone and Android apps are on the way.

My gym isn't on Lift'd yet. Now what?

Start it! Add your gym in a few seconds and you'll get an invite link to share wherever your gym's people already are: group chats, Instagram, or the folks you see every day. Every gym's community starts with one founding member.

I've never really been to a gym. Is Lift'd for me?

Yes — that's who we built it for first. Filter clips to beginner level to learn the basics, check a lift's form right from your workout log before you try it, and log your sets so you can see yourself getting stronger week to week. When you're ready, your gym's community is a place to ask questions and find someone to train with.

How do I know I'm doing an exercise right?

Watch the exercise's form clip before your set. Each one has short coaching cues on screen, like where to set your feet or how far to lower the weight. Start light, focus on the cues, and add weight once the movement feels smooth. If something hurts (not just hard work), stop and ask a trainer at your gym.

Can I share my PRs outside Lift'd?

Yes. Every personal record can be turned into a story-sized PR card you can share to Instagram, TikTok, or your group chats, or post to your gym's feed on Lift'd.

Is my information private and safe?

Your workouts and food log are private to you. Your profile, posts, and gym chat are visible to signed-in members, and you can report or block anyone in a tap.
