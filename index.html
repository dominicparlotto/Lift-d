FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Lift'd free?

Yes. Lift'd is free to use. Create an account and start logging workouts and meals right away.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Lift'd runs in your web browser on your phone or computer. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen to get an app icon. Native iPhone and Android apps are planned.

Is Lift'd good for beginners?

Yes. You choose your experience level when you sign up, the exercise library covers every major muscle group, and form clips can be filtered to beginner level.

How does finding workout partners work?

Join your gym's community to chat with other members and see who's training right now. You can add people as friends, and you're only connected once they add you back.

Where is my data saved?

For now, your account and history are saved in the browser on the device you sign up with, and your password is stored as a secure hash, never as plain text. Syncing across devices is coming soon.
