Support

We usually reply within 1–2 days
Contact us
  • Email support@joinliftd.com with your username and what happened.
  • Or, in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Send Feedback.
  • To report a post, comment, message or account, use Report on it in the app. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Your account

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to choose a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check spam or promotions, then try signing up again with the same email. Still nothing? Email us and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, posts, photos, videos, messages and logs. It can't be undone. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

My account was suspended

Accounts that break our Terms of Use can be banned. If you think we made a mistake, email us.

Safety

  • Block: use ⋯ → Block on someone's profile, post or message. You won't see each other's posts or messages, and any follows between you are removed. Unblock in Profile → Settings → Blocked Accounts.
  • Private account: Profile → Settings → Private Account. Only followers you approve can see your posts.
  • Message requests: messages from people you don't follow wait in Messages → Requests until you accept them.
  • If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Notifications

Turn them on in Profile → Settings → Push Notifications. If they're blocked, in the iPhone app go to the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Lift'd and allow them. On the website on iPhone, add Lift'd to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Your data

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and who can see it. No ads, and we never sell your data.
